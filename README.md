# Kume-Gatdor-Manguet-
Artist 
Dr'Son South Sudanese artist also known as Kume Gatdor Manguet, The son of Dr.Gideon Gatdor, known for his talents as an artist, writter, editorKuma Gatdor Manguet Nhial (also known as Kume Gideon Gatdor) is a young South Sudanese visual artist and content creator based in Juba, South Sudan.
He is active across various digital platforms, including his Facebook Profile and his personal YouTube Channel, where he showcases his artwork and connects with the local South Sudanese creative community. He has also been involved as an administrative team member for local digital music groups like the Shine Light Music platform
